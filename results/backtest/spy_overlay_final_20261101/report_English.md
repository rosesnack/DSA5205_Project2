Among the twelve previously evaluated exploratory SPY-core sector-overlay configurations, GBDT S1 with 15 bps entry and 7.5 bps exit thresholds achieved the highest test-period net CAGR. From 4 January 2023 to 30 September 2026, its net CAGR was 22.72%, compared with 22.02% for SPY with dividends reinvested and 21.67% for fixed-share SPY. The annualized return difference versus reinvestment SPY was 0.70 percentage points, with 2.16% higher terminal wealth, tracking error of 1.24%, an information ratio of 0.50, and maximum drawdown of -19.30%. All figures include the specified execution, stock-borrow and financing costs. The final presentation configuration was selected ex post using observed test performance, so these results are exploratory and are not an independent, untouched holdout estimate. All twelve candidate results are retained.
